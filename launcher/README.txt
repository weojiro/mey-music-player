mey music player
================

1. Double-click "MMP Launcher.bat".
2. Wait until it says "Installation complete!". It installs .NET (only if it is missing) and the app,
   and puts a "mey music player" shortcut on your desktop.
3. Start the app with that shortcut.

Updates are offered inside the app. If the app ever says that .NET is missing,
run "MMP Launcher.bat" again.

The first time, Windows may warn about an unknown publisher. Choose "More info" and then "Run anyway".
